Allow access to your messages

macOS keeps messages and iPhone backups private. You need to allow access once.

  1. Click Open Privacy Settings.
  2. Find Terminal in the list and switch it on.
    Not in the list? Click +, choose Applications → Utilities → Terminal, then click Open.
  3. When macOS says Terminal has to quit, click Later.
  4. Come back here and click Restart and continue.
  5. Quit Terminal (⌘Q), then double-click Start iMessage Search again. You'll return right here.

Sync your messages to this Mac

Turn on Messages in iCloud on both devices. Your messages then download to this Mac.

On your iPhone

  1. Open Settings, tap your name, then tap iCloud.
  2. Tap See All, then Messages, and turn on Use on this iPhone.
  3. In Settings → Apps → Messages, set Keep Messages to Forever.

On this Mac

  1. and sign in with the same Apple Account as your iPhone.
  2. Choose Messages → Settings → iMessage, check Enable Messages in iCloud, and click Sync Now.
  3. Leave Messages open. A first sync can take a while if you have lots of messages.
